Why Cold Immersion

Recovery that feels grounded, not extreme.

01

Restore energy

Cold immersion may support recovery, reduce fatigue, and help you feel more alert after a demanding day or training block.

02

Calm the system

Many guests use the plunge as a practical reset for stress, mental load, and nervous system activation.

03

Sharpen focus

The experience can help support mental clarity and a more intentional pace before the next block of work or training.

04

Ease stiffness

Cold exposure can contribute to a sense of physical reset after workouts, long commutes, or long periods of inactivity.
